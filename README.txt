LABAN SONG GENERATOR
====================
Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox (double-click it). No server or internet needed
(except for the optional Google font).

index.html  - page structure
style.css   - design
script.js   - singing engine, instruments, MP3 encoder, progress, download
assets/js   - lame.min.js (MP3 encoder) and jszip.min.js
assets/img  - favicon.svg

Tip: spell words the way they sound and pick the lyrics language (Bantu or English).
